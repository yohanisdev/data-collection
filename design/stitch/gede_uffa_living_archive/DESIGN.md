## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a balance between scholarly rigor, living cultural heritage, and modern computational linguistics. Designed for the Gede’uffa Language Project, the visual language honors the ancestral agroforestry landscape of the Gedeo highlands while providing an uncompromised, modern digital workspace for community contributors, linguists, and AI researchers.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Editorial Archival**:
- **Literary and Scholarly**: Grounded in classic typography, structured metadata displays, and quiet confidence rather than fleeting tech tropes.
- **Rooted and Organic**: Utilizing organic earthen greens and natural flax/alabaster tones reminiscent of heirloom parchment, stone markers, and shade-grown coffee canopy foliage.
- **Precision AI Interface**: Clean structural alignments, purposeful data density, meticulous typographic hierarchy, and clear semantic validation states ensure that complex linguistic annotation (phonetic transcriptions, audio spectrogram tagging, dialect cross-referencing) feels calm, accessible, and unhurried.
- **Restraint Over Novelty**: Neon highlights, deep glassmorphism blurs, and harsh brutalist outlines are intentionally avoided. Physicality is expressed through quiet ink contrast, hairline borders, and subtle elevation changes.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the Gedeo zone's natural ecosystem and traditional material culture. Surfaces rely on low-strain warm neutrals that emulate natural reading paper, while deep evergreen tones anchor authority.

### Hierarchy & Application
- **Primary (`#1A382B`, deep shade `#12281E`)**: The anchor of the platform. Used for critical primary actions, global navigation surfaces, editorial header emphasis, and grounding brand elements.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#C59B4B`, deep ochre `#B38634`)**: Derived from traditional woven artifacts and natural mineral pigments. Applied sparingly for active focal points, milestone badges, verification stars, and subtle cultural glyph accents.
- **Tertiary / Success (`#2E6B4F`)**: Forest emerald chosen for verified recordings, peer-reviewed phonetic approvals, and healthy model training statuses.
- **Warning & Pending (`#C2822B`)**: Muted highland amber reserved for translations requiring review, dialect disambiguation, and pending community validations.
- **Critical / Rejected (`#A84232`)**: Soft brick terracotta indicating phonetic anomalies, corrupted audio samples, or flagged contributions without visual alarmism.
- **Canvas & Surface Architecture**:
  - Base Canvas: `#FBF9F5` (Alabaster Warm White) transitioning to `#F5F2EB` (Sunken Canvas / Sidebar Tint).
  - Cards & Workspaces: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Ivory) to provide clear figure-ground separation for data entry.
  - Borders: Warm hairline delimiters (`#E6E1D8` standard, `#D8D2C5` for focused borders).
- **Body & Text Neutral**: `#1F2923` (Charcoal Slate) delivers optimal WCAG AAA contrast against alabaster surfaces without the clinical harshness of pure `#000000`. Secondary metadata uses `#4A564F`.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between historic linguistic heritage and contemporary computational clarity. 

- **Display & Editorial Headers (`Newsreader`)**: Newsreader brings literary warmth and dignity. It is reserved for section leads, long-form community narratives, and high-level platform headers. Its italics are particularly well-suited for dialect annotations, phonetic translations, and traditional proverb pull-quotes.
- **System Interface & Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Neutral, highly legible at micro-scales, and human in tone. It provides clinical precision for audio spectrogram markers, dataset statistics, corpus management tables, and contributor inputs.
- **Diacritics & Extended Glyphs**: When rendering Ethiopic script (Fidel) or International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) annotations alongside Gede'uffa Latin orthography, font fallbacks must prioritize high-uniformity open glyph fonts (`Noto Sans Ethiopic` / `Noto Serif Ethiopic`) with line heights expanded by 10% to prevent accent clipping.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an open, editorial rhythm that discourages dense software clutter and promotes contemplative interaction.

### Grid and Composition
- **Desktop (12 Columns, Max Canvas 1360px)**: 12-column layout with `2rem` gutters and dynamic side margins. Split-view layouts divide evenly between the corpus source document/audio workbench (7 columns) and the linguistic metadata/validation ledger (5 columns).
- **Tablet (8 Columns, 768px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins. Sidebar tools collapse into floating contextual drawers.
- **Mobile (4 Columns, <768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` margins. Audio recorders and review actions fix to a persistent bottom action sheet.

### Spacing Principles
- Generous outer section padding (`space-xl`) prevents user fatigue when transcribing extensive oral histories.
- Component-level spacing follows an 8-point baseline scale with 4-point micro-adjustments for badges and data rows (`space-xs` through `space-md`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids artificial glossy 3D planes in favor of natural paper sheets, debossed physical panels, and low-opacity warm ambient light.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: `#FBF9F5` Alabaster flat plane. All background scrolling content rests here.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Workspaces)**: `#FFFFFF` Crisp Ivory surfaced cards resting on a crisp hairline border (`1px solid #E6E1D8`). Shadows are soft and diffused: `0 1px 3px rgba(31, 41, 35, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(31, 41, 35, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Tools & Menus)**: Audio recording docks, dropdown pickers, and dialect filter panels: `1px solid #D8D2C5` with a warm ambient cast `0 8px 24px rgba(26, 56, 43, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Overlays & Critical Audio Verification)**: Centered inspection panels flanked by an ochre-tinted dark scrim (`rgba(18, 40, 30, 0.4)`): elevated with `0 16px 40px rgba(18, 40, 30, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The geometric structure relies on restrained, tailored curves. Fully rounded pill buttons and razor-sharp brutalist edges are avoided in favor of an architectural, organic shape profile.

- **Standard Containers & Cards**: Set to `0.5rem` (8px), matching the subtle, grounded proportions of bookbinding and physical field notebooks.
- **Modals & Hero Editorial Modules**: Scaled to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) for an inviting, refined presence.
- **Audio Waveform Displays & Spectrogram Wrappers**: Set to `0.5rem` with inner hairline clipping masks to preserve clean visual data readouts.
- **Badges & Status Pills**: Constrained to `rounded-sm` (4px) or `rounded` (8px), strictly avoiding circular pill capsules to maintain an archival, typographically aligned aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Forest Green (`#1A382B`) fill with Crisp Ivory text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` corner radius, `12px 24px` padding. On hover, background shifts smoothly to `#12281E` with a faint warm amber drop line (`0 2px 8px rgba(197, 155, 75, 0.2)`).
- **Secondary / Outline**: Transparent fill, `1px solid #D8D2C5`, Charcoal Slate text (`#1F2923`). On hover, surface shifts to `#F5F2EB` and border transitions to `#1A382B`.
- **Accent (Record / Contribute)**: Rich Ochre (`#C59B4B`) with Deep Forest text (`#12281E`), designed exclusively for primary audio submissions and landmark contributions.

### Chips & Semantic Badges
- **Status Chips**: Background tints at 10% opacity with 100% solid text:
  - Approved/Corpus Verified: Background `#2E6B4F1A`, Text `#2E6B4F`, Border `#2E6B4F33`.
  - Dialect Variation / In Review: Background `#C2822B1A`, Text `#C2822B`, Border `#C2822B33`.
  - Disputed / Needs Retake: Background `#A842321A`, Text `#A84232`, Border `#A8423233`.
- **Form & Typography**: All badges use `label-sm` in all-caps with `0.06em` tracking, wrapped in an 8px border-radius with `4px 10px` interior padding.

### Linguistic Audio Cards
- Ivory white container (`#FFFFFF`) with `1px solid #E6E1D8`. Left border highlights the entry category using a 3px accent stroke (Deep Forest Green for primary phrases, Ochre for proverbs/oral folklore).
- Includes an embedded audio waveform container rendered in forest green tones (`#1A382B` active bars, `#E6E1D8` unplayed track), integrated with inline IPA phonetic notation in `Newsreader` italic.

### Editorial Tables (Lexicon & Dataset Explorer)
- Header row: Clean border-bottom (`2px solid #1A382B`), alabaster tint background (`#F5F2EB`), uppercase label tokens (`label-md`).
- Data rows: Alternating subtle hover states (`#FBF9F5` to `#F5F2EB`), cell borders set to hairline `#E6E1D8`, text vertically centered with ample breathing room (`16px 20px` cell padding).

### Input Fields & Annotation Textareas
- Surface: Crisp Ivory (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px solid #D8D2C5` resting border.
- Active/Focused: Border shifts to Deep Forest Green (`#1A382B`) accompanied by a subtle 2px ring in warm ochre (`rgba(197, 155, 75, 0.2)`).
- Placeholder: `#4A564F` at 60% opacity.
- Phonetic Input Mode: Integrated toggle bar allowing keyboard switching between standard Latin orthography, IPA, and Ethiopic Fidel with an understated warm tab indicator.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Custom square markers with rounded corners (`3px` radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios).
- Resting state: `1.5px solid #D8D2C5` on Ivory.
- Checked state: `#1A382B` solid fill with an ochre (`#C59B4B`) checkmark glyph.
